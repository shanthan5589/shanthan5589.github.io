Two-stage pipeline · Medical imaging

Mitosis
Detector

A two-stage pipeline that takes a whole-slide pathology image and outputs precise coordinates of mitotic figures, automating a task pathologists currently perform manually. By combining YOLO and an EfficientNet-B2 classifier, it distinguishes true mitotic figures across unseen slides with different staining characteristics. Achieved F1 0.774 on 11 fully held-out test slides.
